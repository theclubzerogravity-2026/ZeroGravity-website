About the Club

ZeroGravity is the official club of the Department of Information Technology, Smt. Kashibai Navale College of Engineering. We provide a platform where students transform ideas into practical skills through innovation, collaboration, leadership, and real-world experiences.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between classroom learning and industry expectations by organizing technical, professional, and community-driven initiatives throughout the academic year. Whether you're an aspiring developer, designer, leader, or innovator, ZeroGravity offers opportunities to learn, compete, and grow beyond the curriculum.

What We Do

  • 🚀 Technology Sessions — Hands-on workshops on emerging technologies, AI, cybersecurity, cloud computing, web development, and modern software tools.
  • 💼 Career & Placement Readiness — Expert talks, resume-building sessions, mock interviews, aptitude preparation, and guidance from industry professionals.
  • 💡 Innovation Challenges — Project competitions, ideathons, coding contests, hackathons, and technical showcases that encourage creativity and problem-solving.
  • 🎯 TechnoSpark — Our flagship experience where technology meets team spirit through technical challenges, indoor games, sports activities, fun competitions, and collaborative events that strengthen leadership, teamwork, and communication.
  • 🌟 Student Development — Leadership opportunities, event management experience, networking, mentoring, and personality development through club activities.
